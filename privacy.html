Privacy Policy

Skylit Piano (Android app). Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Skylit Piano app ("the app") handles and why. The app is developed by SonicCrazy ("we", "us").

Information we collect

The app lets you play a piano and use online features. Depending on how you use the app, we may handle:

  • Information you provide: for example a display name or username, if the app asks for one.
  • Technical information: such as device type, Android version, app version, language and IP address, which are needed to connect to our servers and keep the app working.
  • Usage and crash information: basic data that helps us find and fix problems.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, precise location or microphone recordings.

How we use information

  • To run the app and its online features
  • To fix bugs and improve the app
  • To reply to your support requests
  • To keep the app safe and prevent misuse

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We only share information with service providers that help us run the app (for example hosting or crash reporting), and only as needed for that purpose. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. To ask us to delete the data linked to you, email skylitpiano@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we can.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email skylitpiano@gmail.com.
